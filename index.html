THE RULES

I. Modifications

No external modifications that give an unfair advantage (e.g. x-ray, freecam, moving in inventory, or mouse tweaks). Allowed mods include Litematica, Xaero's Minimap, and Worldmap.

II. Hacking & Exploits

No hacking or exploiting, including any sort of hack client. We have an active anticheat. You must contact support immediately if you discover an exploit, glitch, or bug. Any gain, even accidental, must be reported.

III. Griefing

Griefing is allowed; however, it is the people's choice what to do and not up to rules or moderators.

IV. Punishment Circumvention

Users are not allowed to avoid or bypass punishments. Any circumvention with alt accounts may result in a ban on all accounts. Use of server-chat while muted/banned is prohibited.

V. Complicity

Players involved in breaking rules (assisting, participating, or benefiting) may be punished as if they committed the act themselves.

VI. Server Disruption

Intentionally lagging the server, intervening in staff operations, DOS/DDOS attacks, or spamming tickets is strictly prohibited. Mass spawning of entities (boats, minecarts, items) that causes lag will result in automatic despawning.

VII. Personal Information (Doxxing)

Sharing personal information of others (names, photos, videos, phone numbers, locations) is strictly prohibited. The threat to use personal information is also banned regardless of intent.

VIII. Impersonation & Defamation

Impersonating players or staff via usernames, skins, or nicknames is banned. Spreading false information (defamation), faking evidence, or making unfounded allegations (e.g. pedophilia) will result in severe punishment.

IX. Compliance

All named items, titles, nicknames, and Discord accounts must be compliant with Rule X and must be presented in English characters (no accents/symbols).

X. Respect & Toxicity

Spamming, harassment, bullying, and hate speech are not tolerated. This includes:

  • Discriminatory language towards racial, social, or religious identities.
  • Encouraging self-harm or Nazism.
  • Sexual or explicit content.
  • Political roleplay/larping involving ongoing real-world conflicts (e.g. Ukraine, Russia, Palestine, Israel).
